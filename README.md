## Product range

- Anti-theft locks for iPad, tablets, MacBook
- Wall mounts, counter mounts, floor stands
- Vehicle tablet holders for fleet management
- Retail kiosks and POS solutions

## About

ElockStore is a French e-commerce dedicated to professional security solutions for tablets and laptops. Used by retail chains, hotels, hospitals, museums, transport companies and SMBs across Europe and the United Kingdom. Reseller of leading brands: Maclocks, Compulocks, ARKON, Brodit.
